Row buttons: ⤓ re-fill stars below · ＋ insert a day below (all rows below move down 1 day) · ✕ delete day (rows below move up). Changing a row's date shifts all rows below by the same amount. Dates with two stars or no star are set under “Exceptions” when generating (bookings for both stars go on that row).

Bookings repeat every month. By star: if a star occurs twice in the month, the pooja goes on its last occurrence. By weekday: “first” = the first such weekday of the Malayalam month; “every” = all of them.
